02 / LEARNING OUTCOMES

By the end of this course, students can:

  1. Describe technology entrepreneurship in the context of building an effective business model.
  2. Analyze how entrepreneurs use technology to establish new startup businesses.
  3. Solve technology entrepreneurship problems through business analysis and design.
  4. Apply technology business models to expand into international markets.
  5. Implement a model to improve technology entrepreneurship and adaptation to real-world business.
