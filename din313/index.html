About the course

Human needs meet digital innovation

Introduction to design thinking. Digital landscape. Business goals and challenges. Empathy and user-centricity. Defining problem statements. Ideation and brainstorming. Prototyping for design thinking in digital transformation. User testing and feedback. Iterative design. Technology digital agile project management. Implementation and deployment. Continuous improvement. Measuring success and ROI. Guest/Selected topic and presentation.
