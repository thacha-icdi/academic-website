02 / LEARNING OUTCOMES

By the end of this course, students can:

  1. Describe design thinking in digital transformation.
  2. Analyze business data related to design thinking in digital transformation.
  3. Solve problems involving design thinking in digital transformation.
  4. Apply design thinking in digital transformation to improve business processes.
  5. Implement a design thinking model that helps new entrepreneurs apply modern knowledge in real-world business.
