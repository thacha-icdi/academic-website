Find data sources for your research paper

Enter your research keyword to find matching datasets with CSV, JSON or Excel data files.

Search for Data Sources

Enter a keyword and click Find to search for matching datasets.

Optional year range: dataset publication year, or repository/catalogue creation year where publication date is unavailable. This does not filter the years covered by the data. Records without a known year are excluded when a year range is selected.

Search for Reference (Elsevier, Crossref, Google Scholar)

Enter keywords to find research references. Crossref shows article metadata here; Other sources open their own search results with your keywords and selected years.

Search for Research Title

Enter 3–4 keywords or short phrases in separate boxes to find existing research article titles containing all your keywords.

Matching Datasets (up to 10)

Searches: Figshare, Data.gov, Thailand Open Data, Data.gov.uk, Data.gov.au, Zenodo, Harvard Dataverse, World Bank and Hugging Face, plus a small verified UCI catalogue. Only whole-word keyword matches with CSV, JSON or Excel access are shown. Source availability is reported above; fewer than 10 results may be found. Review documentation and licensing before use.

Using a data source

Open a dataset link to view its data files and documentation. Check the original dataset's license, variables, collection method and access conditions before using it.
